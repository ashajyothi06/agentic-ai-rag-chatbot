# Sample Queries

Use these after ingestion to demonstrate the chatbot.

1. **What is Agentic AI and how is it different from traditional AI systems?**
2. **What are the key characteristics or capabilities of agentic AI systems?**
3. **What role do planning and reasoning play in Agentic AI?**
4. **What are some applications or use cases of Agentic AI mentioned in the eBook?**
5. **What challenges or risks are associated with Agentic AI?**
6. **How does the eBook describe the future or business impact of Agentic AI?**

## Out-of-scope grounding test

Ask a question that is not covered by the eBook, for example:

> Who won the latest FIFA World Cup?

Expected behavior: the chatbot should refuse to answer from outside knowledge and say that it could not find sufficient information in the provided eBook.

> Note: exact wording for in-scope answers depends on the PDF passages retrieved at runtime.
